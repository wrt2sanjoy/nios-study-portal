Day 1 (Friday, Oct 30): The Easiest 17 Marks

  • Topics:
  • Trigonometric Values Table ($\sin, \cos, \tan$ for $0^\circ, 30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$).
  • Heights & Distances (5 Marks Sure-Shot!): Tower, light house, angle of elevation/depression.
  • Probability (Ch 26 - 5 Marks): Coins, 1 or 2 dice, cards, balls in a bag.
  • Goal: Complete these 2 topics tonight. You will have locked in 17 marks on Day 1!
  • Read:
  • 04_GUARANTEED_SURE_SHOT_LONG_QUESTIONS.md (Template 1: Heights & Distances)
  • 02_ALL_IN_ONE_FORMULA_CHEAT_SHEET.md (Trigonometry & Probability section)

Day 2 (Saturday, Oct 31): Algebra Powerhouse (15 Marks)

  • Topics:
  • Arithmetic Progression (AP - Ch 7):
    • $n$-th term: $a_n = a + (n-1)d$
    • Sum: $S_n = \frac{n}{2}[2a + (n-1)d]$
  • Quadratic Equations (Ch 6):
    • Discriminant $D = b^2 - 4ac$ (Nature of roots: $D > 0, D = 0, D < 0$)
    • Finding roots using formula $x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{D}}{2a}$
  • Special Products & Factorisation (Ch 4):
    • $(a+b)^2, (a-b)^2, a^2 - b^2, a^3 + b^3, a^3 - b^3$.
  • Goal: Solve the 8 standard problems in 05_CHAPTER_BY_CHAPTER_SUPER_SUMMARY.md.

Day 3 (Sunday, Nov 1 - Weekend Deep Work): The 5-Mark Graph & Geometry (15 Marks)

  • Topics:
  • Linear Equations Graphical Solution (5 Marks Sure-Shot!):
    • Plotting $a_1 x + b_1 y = c_1$ and $a_2 x + b_2 y = c_2$ on a graph sheet, finding intersection point $(x, y)$.
  • Circle Tangents & Angles (Ch 16 & 17):
    • Tangent $\perp$ radius ($OP \perp PT$).
    • Tangents from external point are equal ($PA = PB$).
    • Angle in semicircle is $90^\circ$; Cyclic quadrilateral opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$.
  • Construction (Ch 18 - 3 to 5 Marks):
    • Tangents to a circle from an external point using compass and ruler.
  • Goal: Practice drawing 2 graphs and 2 circle tangent constructions on paper!

Day 4 (Monday, Nov 2): Statistics & Coordinate Geometry (15 Marks)

  • Topics:
  • Statistics (Ch 25):
    • Grouped data Median: $L + \left(\frac{\frac{N}{2} - cf}{f}\right) \times h$
    • Grouped data Mean: $\bar{x} = \frac{\sum f_i x_i}{\sum f_i}$
    • Empirical relation: $\text{Mode} = 3 \text{Median} - 2 \text{Mean}$
  • Coordinate Geometry (Ch 19):
    • Distance formula: $d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2}$
    • Mid-point formula: $\left(\frac{x_1 + x_2}{2}, \frac{y_1 + y_2}{2}\right)$
    • Section formula: $\left(\frac{m x_2 + n x_1}{m+n}, \frac{m y_2 + n y_1}{m+n}\right)$
  • Goal: Solve 2 median problems and 3 coordinate geometry problems.

Day 5 (Tuesday, Nov 3): Mensuration & Commercial Maths (12 Marks)

  • Topics:
  • Surface Area & Volume (Ch 21):
    • Cylinder: $V = \pi r^2 h$, $\text{CSA} = 2\pi rh$, $\text{TSA} = 2\pi r(r+h)$
    • Cone: $V = \frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$, $l = \sqrt{r^2+h^2}$, $\text{CSA} = \pi rl$
    • Sphere & Hemisphere: $V = \frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, $\text{TSA} = 4\pi r^2$
  • Installment Buying (Ch 9):
    • Cash price, Down payment, Balance owed, Interest calculation.
  • Goal: Master formula substitutions for direct 2-mark and 3-mark questions.

Day 6 (Wednesday, Nov 4): The Objective Blitz & Rehearsal (Crucial Day!)

  • Topics:
  • Go through 03_TOP_50_REPEATED_OBJECTIVES_MCQ_SOLVED.md from start to finish.
  • Attempt the 85-mark 06_PREDICTED_MOCK_TEST_PAPER_2026.md under timed conditions (or self-check answers).
  • Pack your exam kit: Admit card/hall ticket, ID proof, Geometry box (ruler, compass, sharp pencil, eraser), black/blue pens.

Day 7 (Thursday, Nov 5 - Exam Day Morning):

  • 45-minute calm morning revision:
  • Read through 02_ALL_IN_ONE_FORMULA_CHEAT_SHEET.md.
  • Remember: You only need 28 to pass, but your target is 50–65+.
  • In the exam room: First 15 minutes, tick all the objective questions you know directly, then solve the 5-mark Heights & Distance and Graphical Linear equation. Walk in with full confidence!
