Section B Detailed Solutions

Q29:
Use identity $(a+b)^2 - (a-b)^2 = 4ab$.
Here $a = 2x, b = 5y \implies 4(2x)(5y) = \mathbf{40xy}$.

Q30:
$a = 3, b = -5, c = 2$.
$D = (-5)^2 - 4(3)(2) = 25 - 24 = \mathbf{1}$.
Since $D > 0$, the roots are real and distinct (unequal).

Q31:
$a = 4, d = 5, n = 20$.
$a_{20} = a + (20 - 1)d = 4 + 19(5) = 4 + 95 = \mathbf{99}$.

Q32:
In right $\Delta OAP$, $\angle OAP = 90^\circ$.
By Pythagoras: $PA = \sqrt{OP^2 - OA^2} = \sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = \sqrt{100 - 36} = \sqrt{64} = \mathbf{8\text{ cm}}$.

Q33:
Opposite angles sum to $180^\circ$:
$(2x + 10) + (3x + 20) = 180 \implies 5x + 30 = 180 \implies 5x = 150 \implies \mathbf{x = 30}$.
$\angle A = 2(30) + 10 = \mathbf{70^\circ}$.

Q34:
$m : n = 2 : 1$, $A(1, 3), B(4, 6)$.
$x = \frac{2(4) + 1(1)}{2 + 1} = \frac{8 + 1}{3} = \frac{9}{3} = 3$.
$y = \frac{2(6) + 1(3)}{2 + 1} = \frac{12 + 3}{3} = \frac{15}{3} = 5$.
Point is $\mathbf{(3, 5)}$.

Q35:
$\text{Area} = \frac{\theta}{360} \pi r^2 = \frac{60}{360} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 6 \times 6 = \frac{1}{6} \times \frac{22}{7} \times 36 = \frac{132}{7} \approx \mathbf{18.86\text{ cm}^2}$.

Q36:
Denominator $= \sin^2 30^\circ + \cos^2 30^\circ = 1$.
Numerator $= 5\left(\frac{1}{2}\right)^2 + 4\left(\frac{2}{\sqrt{3}}\right)^2 - (1)^2 = 5\left(\frac{1}{4}\right) + 4\left(\frac{4}{3}\right) - 1 = \frac{5}{4} + \frac{16}{3} - 1 = \frac{15 + 64 - 12}{12} = \mathbf{\frac{67}{12}}$.

Q37:
(i) Red Kings $= 2 \implies P = \frac{2}{52} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{26}}$.
(ii) Spades $= 13 \implies P = \frac{13}{52} = \mathbf{\frac{1}{4}}$.

Q38:
$x^3 - (2y)^3 = (x - 2y)(x^2 + 2xy + (2y)^2) = \mathbf{(x - 2y)(x^2 + 2xy + 4y^2)}$.

Q39:
Let ratio be $k : 1$.
Using x-coordinate: $-1 = \frac{k(6) + 1(-3)}{k + 1} \implies -(k + 1) = 6k - 3 \implies -k - 1 = 6k - 3 \implies 7k = 2 \implies k = \frac{2}{7}$.
Ratio is $\mathbf{2 : 7}$.

Q40:
Radius $r = 3.5\text{ cm}, OP = 6.5\text{ cm}$.
Length of tangent $= \sqrt{6.5^2 - 3.5^2} = \sqrt{(6.5 - 3.5)(6.5 + 3.5)} = \sqrt{3 \times 10} = \sqrt{30} \approx \mathbf{5.48\text{ cm}}$.

Q41:
Volume of cylinder $= \text{Volume of sphere}$
$\pi r_1^2 h = \frac{4}{3}\pi r_2^3 \implies (4.2)^2 \times 10 = \frac{4}{3} r_2^3$
$17.64 \times 10 = \frac{4}{3} r_2^3 \implies 176.4 \times \frac{3}{4} = r_2^3 \implies 132.3 = r_2^3 \implies r_2 \approx \mathbf{5.1\text{ cm}}$.

Q42:
Total $N = 5 + 8 + 15 + 12 + 10 = 50 \implies \frac{N}{2} = 25$.
Cumulative frequencies: $5, 13, 28, 40, 50$.
Median class is $20 - 30$ ($L = 20, cf = 13, f = 15, h = 10$).
$\text{Median} = 20 + \frac{25 - 13}{15} \times 10 = 20 + \frac{12}{15} \times 10 = 20 + 8 = \mathbf{28}$.

Q43:
- Equation 1 ($x + y = 5$): $(0, 5), (5, 0), (2, 3)$.
- Equation 2 ($2x - y = 4$): $(0, -4), (2, 0), (3, 2)$.
Intersection point: $x = 3, y = 2 \implies \mathbf{(3, 2)}$.
Vertices of triangle formed with y-axis ($x = 0$):
Line 1 hits y-axis at $(0, 5)$; Line 2 hits y-axis at $(0, -4)$; Intersection is $(3, 2)$.
Vertices: $\mathbf{(0, 5), \; (0, -4), \; (3, 2)}$.

Q44:
Let pole be $AB = 60\text{ m}$.
- Car 1 at $C$ (elevation $45^\circ$): $\tan 45^\circ = \frac{60}{BC} \implies BC = 60\text{ m}$.
- Car 2 at $D$ (elevation $30^\circ$): $\tan 30^\circ = \frac{60}{BD} \implies \frac{1}{\sqrt{3}} = \frac{60}{BD} \implies BD = 60\sqrt{3} = 60 \times 1.732 = 103.92\text{ m}$.
Total distance between cars:
$CD = BC + BD = 60 + 103.92 = \mathbf{163.92\text{ m}}$.
