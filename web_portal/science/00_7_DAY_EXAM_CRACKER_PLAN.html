Day 1: High-Yield Physics — Light & Human Eye (15 Marks)

  • Target Chapters: L.15 (Light Energy)
  • Core Topics:
  • Reflection & Refraction: Snell's Law ($\frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \mu$).
  • Mirror Formula ($\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$) and Lens Formula ($\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$).
  • Power of Lens: $P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (in metres)}}$ (Unit: Dioptre, $\text{D}$).
  • Eye Defects: Myopia (corrected by concave lens) vs Hypermetropia (corrected by convex lens) with ray diagrams.
  • Practice: Draw the two eye correction diagrams on paper!

Day 2: High-Yield Physics — Electricity & Magnetism (15 Marks)

  • Target Chapters: L.16 (Electrical Energy) & L.17 (Magnetic Effect)
  • Core Topics:
  • Ohm's Law: $V = IR$. Circuit diagram for verification.
  • Equivalent resistance: Series ($R_s = R_1 + R_2 + R_3$) vs Parallel ($\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3}$).
  • Electric Power ($P = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R}$) and Commercial Energy billing ($1\text{ kWh} = 1\text{ unit} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$).
  • Fleming's Left-Hand Rule (Electric Motor) & Domestic Circuit Safety (Earth wire, Fuse).
  • Practice: Solve 2 numerical problems on equivalent resistance and energy cost.

Day 3 (Weekend): Biology Core — Life Processes & Heredity (18 Marks)

  • Target Chapters: L.22 (Life Processes I), L.23 (Control), L.24 (Reproduction), L.25 (Heredity)
  • Core Topics:
  • Human Heart & Double Circulation (Why is oxygenated separated from deoxygenated blood?).
  • Nephron: Ultrafiltration and urine formation.
  • Aerobic vs Anaerobic respiration pathways.
  • Mendel's Monohybrid Cross: $3:1$ ratio and Punnett square.
  • Sex determination in humans: Father ($XY$) determines gender of child.
  • Practice: Sketch the heart diagram and the Mendel $3:1$ cross chart.

Day 4 (Weekend): Chemistry Core — Salts, Metals & Carbon (16 Marks)

  • Target Chapters: L.8 (Acids, Bases & Salts), L.27 (Metals & Non-metals), L.28 (Carbon Compounds)
  • Core Topics:
  • Preparation and 2 uses of: Bleaching Powder, Baking Soda, Washing Soda, Plaster of Paris.
  • pH scale: Acids ($<7$), Neutral ($7$), Bases ($>7$).
  • Reactivity series & displacement reactions.
  • Carbon: Catenation, Tetravalency, Homologous series, and Cleansing action of soap (Micelles).
  • Practice: Write down the 4 salt preparation chemical equations twice.

Day 5: Mechanics, Gravitation & Periodic Table (12 Marks)

  • Target Chapters: L.10 (Force & Motion), L.11 (Gravitation), L.5 & L.6 (Atoms & Periodic Table)
  • Core Topics:
  • Newton's Second Law: $F = ma$ and momentum ($p = mv$).
  • Universal Law of Gravitation: $F = G \frac{M m}{r^2}$. Difference between $g$ (acceleration due to gravity) and $G$ (Universal constant).
  • Mass (constant, scalar) vs Weight ($W = mg$, variable, vector).
  • Modern Periodic Table: Periodic trends across periods (left to right) and down groups (atomic size, valency, metallic character).

Day 6: Ecology & Top 50 Objectives Blitz (42 Marks!)

  • Target Chapters: L.29 (Natural Environment), L.30 (Human Impact), and all objective questions.
  • Core Topics:
  • 10% Law of Energy Transfer (Lindeman's rule) in food chain.
  • Ozone layer depletion: Reaction of Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs).
  • Biomagnification of pesticides (DDT).
