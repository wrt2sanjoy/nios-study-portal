Section B Detailed Solutions

Q29:
Newton's Second Law: The rate of change of momentum of a body is directly proportional to the applied unbalanced force and takes place in the direction of the force.
Formula: $F = ma = \frac{m(v - u)}{t}$.

Q30:
1. Mass: Quantity of matter in a body; constant everywhere; scalar unit $\text{kg}$.
2. Weight: Gravitational pull $W = mg$; varies from place to place; vector unit $\text{N}$.

Q31:
$u = -30\text{ cm}, f = -15\text{ cm}$.
$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u} \implies \frac{1}{v} = \frac{1}{f} - \frac{1}{u} = \frac{1}{-15} - \frac{1}{-30} = -\frac{1}{15} + \frac{1}{30} = -\frac{1}{30}$.
$\mathbf{v = -30\text{ cm}}$. The image is real, inverted, and formed at the centre of curvature.

Q32:
1 Dioptre is the power of a lens whose focal length is 1 metre ($1\text{ D} = 1\text{ m}^{-1}$).
A power of $-1.5\text{ D}$ indicates a Concave Lens (diverging lens).

Q33:
$\text{Ca(OH)}_2 + \text{Cl}_2 \to \text{CaOCl}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O}$.
Use: As a disinfectant for water purification or bleaching agent in textile mills.

Q34:
Sodium is an extremely reactive alkali metal. It reacts vigorously with atmospheric oxygen and water vapour, producing hydrogen gas and catching fire. To prevent this, it is stored submerged under kerosene oil.

Q35:
An homologous series is a group of organic compounds sharing the same functional group and general formula, where consecutive members differ by a $-\text{CH}_2-$ unit ($14\text{ u}$).
First two alkanes: Methane ($\text{CH}_4$) and Ethane ($\text{C}_2\text{H}_6$).

Q36:
Neuron diagram consists of:
- Dendrites (receive signals)
- Cyton / Cell body (contains nucleus)
- Axon (transmits nerve impulses)
- Nerve endings (synaptic transmission).

Q37:
10% Law: During energy transfer across trophic levels in an ecosystem, only about 10% of the energy is stored as biomass in the next level; the remaining 90% is dissipated as heat or used in respiration.

Q38:
Glucose ($6\text{C}$) breaks down in cytoplasm into Pyruvate ($3\text{C}$):
1. In Yeast (Absence of $\text{O}_2$): Ethanol + $\text{CO}_2$ + $2\text{ ATP}$.
2. In Muscle Cells (Lack of $\text{O}_2$): Lactic acid + Energy (causes muscle fatigue).
3. In Mitochondria (Presence of $\text{O}_2$): $\text{CO}_2$ + $\text{H}_2\text{O}$ + $38\text{ ATP}$.

Q39:
Formula: $\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$.
Preparation: $\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot 2\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow{373\text{ K}} \text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O} + 1\frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$.
Uses: 1. Bone fracture casts. 2. Statues and false ceilings.

Q40:
Cross: $TT \times tt \to F_1: Tt$ (All Tall).
Selfing: $Tt \times Tt \to F_2: TT, Tt, Tt, tt$.
Phenotypic ratio: $3 : 1$ (3 Tall : 1 Dwarf). Genotypic ratio: $1 : 2 : 1$.

Q41:
(a) Electric power is the rate at which electrical energy is consumed in an electric circuit: $P = VI = I^2 R$.
(b) Heat $H = I^2 Rt = (5)^2 \times 20 \times 30 = 25 \times 600 = \mathbf{15,000\text{ Joules}}$ ($15\text{ kJ}$).

Q42:
1. Roasting: Heating sulphide ores in excess air:
$2\text{ZnS} + 3\text{O}_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2\text{ZnO} + 2\text{SO}_2$.
2. Calcination: Heating carbonate ores in limited/no air:
$\text{ZnCO}_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{ZnO} + \text{CO}_2$.

Q43:
(a) Myopia is short-sightedness. Causes: excessive curvature of lens, elongation of eyeball.
(b) Ray diagrams:
- Defective eye: rays converge in front of retina.
- Corrected eye: concave lens diverges rays to focus sharply on retina.
(OR)
(a) $V = IR$. Circuit diagram with battery, key, ammeter, voltmeter, resistor, rheostat.
(b) Connect $3\,\Omega$ and $6\,\Omega$ in parallel: $\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6} = \frac{1}{2} \implies R_p = 2\,\Omega$.
Then connect with $2\,\Omega$ in series: $R_{\text{total}} = 2 + 2 = \mathbf{4\,\Omega}$.

Q44:
(a) Nephron: Bowman's capsule, Glomerulus, Convoluted tubule, Collecting duct.
(b) 1. Ultrafiltration in glomerulus $\to$ 2. Selective reabsorption of glucose, water, amino acids $\to$ 3. Tubular secretion of wastes.
(OR)
(a) Catenation (carbon bonds with carbon to form chains/rings) and Tetravalency (valency 4 allows bonding with 4 atoms).
(b) Soap molecule has hydrophilic head ($-\text{COO}^-\text{Na}^+$) and hydrophobic tail (hydrocarbon). In water, tails dissolve in central grease droplet while heads face outward, forming a spherical Micelle that lifts dirt into water.
