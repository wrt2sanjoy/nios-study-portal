Part 1: Multiple Choice Questions (1 Mark Each)

1. Physics (Optics, Mechanics & Electricity)

Q1. The SI unit of electric potential difference is:
(A) Ampere
(B) Volt
(C) Ohm
(D) Watt

Answer: (B) Volt
Explanation: $V = W/Q$ (Joules per Coulomb = Volt).

Q2. The commercial unit of electrical energy is kilowatt-hour ($\text{kWh}$). $1\text{ kWh}$ is equal to:
(A) $3.6 \times 10^5\text{ J}$
(B) $3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$
(C) $36 \times 10^6\text{ J}$
(D) $3.6 \times 10^4\text{ J}$

Answer: (B) $3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$
Explanation: $1\text{ kW} \times 1\text{ hr} = 1000\text{ W} \times 3600\text{ s} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ J}$.

Q3. A person cannot see distant objects clearly, but can see nearby objects distinctly. The defect of vision he is suffering from is:
(A) Hypermetropia
(B) Myopia
(C) Presbyopia
(D) Cataract

Answer: (B) Myopia
Explanation: Myopia is short-sightedness, corrected with a concave lens.

Q4. A lens has a power of $+2.0\text{ D}$. Its focal length is:
(A) $+50\text{ cm}$
(B) $-50\text{ cm}$
(C) $+20\text{ cm}$
(D) $+100\text{ cm}$

Answer: (A) +50 cm
Explanation: $f = \frac{1}{P} = \frac{1}{+2} = +0.5\text{ m} = +50\text{ cm}$ (Convex lens).

Q5. If the resistance of a wire of length $l$ is $R$, then another wire of the same material having double the length and same cross-sectional area will have resistance:
(A) $R/2$
(B) $2R$
(C) $4R$
(D) $R$

Answer: (B) 2R
Explanation: $R \propto l$. Doubling length doubles the resistance.

Q6. The acceleration due to gravity on the surface of the Moon is approximately:
(A) $\frac{1}{2}$ that of Earth
(B) $\frac{1}{6}$ that of Earth
(C) 6 times that of Earth
(D) Equal to that of Earth

Answer: (B) 1/6 that of Earth

Q7. An object of mass $10\text{ kg}$ is taken to the Moon. Its mass on the Moon will be:
(A) $10\text{ kg}$
(B) $\frac{10}{6}\text{ kg}$
(C) $60\text{ kg}$
(D) $0\text{ kg}$

Answer: (A) 10 kg
Explanation: Mass is constant everywhere. (Weight changes to $\frac{1}{6}$, but mass remains $10\text{ kg}$).

2. Chemistry (Acids, Salts, Metals & Carbon)

Q8. The chemical formula of Bleaching Powder is:
(A) $\text{CaCl}_2$
(B) $\text{CaOCl}_2$
(C) $\text{Ca(OH)}_2$
(D) $\text{CaSO}_4$

Answer: (B) $\text{CaOCl}_2$ (Calcium oxychloride)

Q9. Which of the following salts is used for supporting fractured bones in medicine?
(A) Bleaching powder
(B) Baking soda
(C) Plaster of Paris
(D) Washing soda

Answer: (C) Plaster of Paris ($\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$)

Q10. The pH of an aqueous solution is measured to be $3$. The solution is:
(A) Strongly alkaline
(B) Weakly alkaline
(C) Strongly acidic
(D) Neutral

Answer: (C) Strongly acidic (pH $< 7$ is acidic; lower pH means higher acidity).

Q11. A metal which is liquid at room temperature is:
(A) Sodium
(B) Mercury
(C) Bromine
(D) Gallium

Answer: (B) Mercury (Hg)
(Note: Bromine is a liquid NON-metal; Mercury is a liquid METAL).

Q12. Which of the following metals is stored under kerosene oil to prevent accidental fire?
(A) Magnesium
(B) Aluminium
(C) Sodium
(D) Iron

Answer: (C) Sodium (Na) (and Potassium).

Q13. The non-metal which is a good conductor of electricity is:
(A) Diamond
(B) Graphite
(C) Sulphur
(D) Phosphorus

Answer: (B) Graphite (Allotrope of carbon with free delocalized electrons).

Q14. The functional group present in an alcohol molecule is:
(A) $-\text{CHO}$
(B) $-\text{COOH}$
(C) $-\text{OH}$
(D) $>\text{C=O}$

Answer: (C) -OH

3. Biology & Environment

Q15. The basic structural and functional filtration unit of the human kidney is:
(A) Neuron
(B) Nephron
(C) Alveolus
(D) Glomerulus

Answer: (B) Nephron

Q16. In humans, fertilization of the egg by a sperm normally takes place in the:
(A) Uterus
(B) Ovary
(C) Fallopian tube (Oviduct)
(D) Vagina

Answer: (C) Fallopian tube (Oviduct)

Q17. According to Lindeman's 10% law, if $1000\text{ J}$ of solar energy is trapped by producers, the energy available to secondary consumers will be:
(A) $100\text{ J}$
(B) $10\text{ J}$
(C) $1\text{ J}$
(D) $0.1\text{ J}$

Answer: (B) 10 J
Explanation: Producers ($1000\text{ J}$) $\to$ Herbivores ($100\text{ J}$) $\to$ Carnivores ($10\text{ J}$).

Part 2: Fill in the Blanks (2 Marks Each / 1 Mark Per Part)

Q18.
(i) Newton's first law of motion is also known as the law of ______.

Answer: inertia

(ii) The product of mass and velocity of a body is called ______.

Answer: momentum ($p = mv$)

Q19.
(i) The device used to measure electric current in a circuit is called ______.

Answer: ammeter

(ii) An ammeter is always connected in ______ in an electrical circuit.

Answer: series (Voltmeter is connected in parallel)

Q20.
(i) The focal length of a plane mirror is ______.

Answer: infinity

(ii) The power of a concave lens is always ______.

Answer: negative

Q21.
(i) The chemical formula of Baking Soda is ______.

Answer: $\text{NaHCO}_3$ (Sodium hydrogen carbonate)

(ii) Heating Gypsum at $373\text{ K}$ produces ______.

Answer: Plaster of Paris ($\text{CaSO}_4 \cdot \frac{1}{2}\text{H}_2\text{O}$)

Q22.
(i) The property of self-linking of carbon atoms through covalent bonds is known as ______.

Answer: catenation

(ii) The general formula of Alkanes is ______.

Answer: $\text{C}_n\text{H}_{2n+2}$

Q23.
(i) The hormone secreted by the pancreas that regulates blood sugar level is ______.

Answer: insulin

(ii) The plant hormone responsible for phototropism (bending towards light) is ______.

Answer: auxin

Q24.
(i) In Mendel's monohybrid cross, the phenotypic ratio in the $F_2$ generation is ______.

Answer: $3 : 1$

(ii) Normal human somatic cells contain ______ pairs of chromosomes.

Answer: 23 (46 chromosomes total)

Q25.
(i) The gas responsible for depleting the stratospheric ozone layer is ______.

Answer: Chlorofluorocarbon (CFC)

(ii) The progressive accumulation of non-biodegradable toxic substances along a food chain is termed ______.

Answer: biomagnification

Part 3: True / False Statements (2 Marks Each / 1 Mark Per Part)

Q26.
(i) "Weight of an object remains constant everywhere in the universe."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: Mass remains constant; weight depends on the local acceleration due to gravity ($W = mg$).

(ii) "Action and reaction forces act on two different bodies."

Answer: TRUE
Reason: Newton's Third Law states forces act on different bodies in opposite directions.

Q27.
(i) "The resistivity of a metallic wire depends on its length and thickness."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: Resistance ($R$) depends on length and area, but resistivity ($\rho$) is an intrinsic material property depending only on the nature of the material and temperature.

(ii) "An electric fuse wire should have high resistance and a low melting point."

Answer: TRUE
Reason: So that it melts quickly and breaks the circuit in case of an overload.

Q28.
(i) "A convex mirror always forms a virtual, erect, and diminished image."

Answer: TRUE
Reason: This is why convex mirrors are used as rear-view mirrors in vehicles.

(ii) "Red light bends the most when passing through a glass prism."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: Violet light bends the most; red light has the longest wavelength and bends the least.

Q29.
(i) "Pure water has a pH equal to 7 at $25^\circ\text{C}$."

Answer: TRUE

(ii) "Acids turn red litmus paper blue."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: Bases turn red litmus blue; acids turn blue litmus red.

Q30.
(i) "Saponification is the chemical reaction used to prepare soaps."

Answer: TRUE

(ii) "Diamond and graphite are chemical isomers of carbon."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: They are allotropes, not isomers.

Q31.
(i) "Deoxygenated blood enters the left atrium of the human heart."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: Deoxygenated blood from the body enters the RIGHT atrium; oxygenated blood enters the LEFT atrium.

(ii) "In humans, the male gamete determines the sex of the unborn child."

Answer: TRUE
Reason: The father donates either an $X$ or $Y$ chromosome, whereas the mother always donates an $X$.

Q32.
(i) "Energy flow in an ecosystem is unidirectional."

Answer: TRUE
Reason: Energy flows from the Sun $\to$ producers $\to$ herbivores $\to$ carnivores and cannot flow backward.

(ii) "Biodegradable substances are the primary cause of biomagnification."

Answer: FALSE
Reason: Non-biodegradable chemicals like DDT cause biomagnification because they cannot be broken down.
