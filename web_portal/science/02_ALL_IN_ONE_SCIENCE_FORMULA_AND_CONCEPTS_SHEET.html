PART 1: PHYSICS FORMULAS & SI UNITS

1. Motion, Force & Gravitation

  • Newton's Second Law: $$F = ma = \frac{m(v - u)}{t}$$ (Force $F$ in Newtons $\text{N}$, mass $m$ in $\text{kg}$, acceleration $a$ in $\text{m/s}^2$)
  • Momentum: $$p = mv \quad (\text{SI Unit: }\text{kg}\cdot\text{m/s})$$
  • Universal Law of Gravitation: $$F = G \frac{m_1 m_2}{r^2}$$ ($G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{kg}^2$ is the Universal Gravitational Constant)
  • Acceleration due to gravity ($g$): $$g = \frac{GM}{R^2} \approx 9.8\text{ m/s}^2 \quad (\text{On Moon, } g_{\text{moon}} = \frac{1}{6} g_{\text{earth}})$$
  • Weight: $$W = mg \quad (\text{Weight is a force, measured in Newtons }\text{N})$$
  • Archimedes' Principle: $\text{Buoyant Force} = \text{Weight of fluid displaced}$.

2. Work, Energy & Power

  • Work Done: $$W = F \times s \quad (\text{SI Unit: Joule, }\text{J})$$ (1 Joule is the work done when a force of $1\text{ N}$ moves a body through a distance of $1\text{ m}$)
  • Kinetic Energy: $$KE = \frac{1}{2}mv^2$$
  • Gravitational Potential Energy: $$PE = mgh$$
  • Power: $$P = \frac{\text{Work}}{\text{Time}} = \frac{W}{t} \quad (\text{SI Unit: Watt, }\text{W})$$

3. Light (Optics)

  • Snell's Law of Refraction: $$\mu = \frac{\sin i}{\sin r} = \text{Constant}$$
  • Mirror Formula (Spherical Mirrors): $$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} + \frac{1}{u}$$
  • Magnification: $m = -\frac{v}{u} = \frac{h_i}{h_o}$
  • Lens Formula (Spherical Lenses): $$\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} - \frac{1}{u}$$
  • Magnification: $m = +\frac{v}{u} = \frac{h_i}{h_o}$
  • Sign Convention (Cartesian):
  • Object distance $u$ is ALWAYS negative ($-$).
  • Concave mirror / Concave lens: focal length $f$ is ALWAYS negative ($-$).
  • Convex mirror / Convex lens: focal length $f$ is ALWAYS positive ($+$).
  • Power of a Lens: $$P = \frac{1}{f\text{ (in metres)}} \quad (\text{SI Unit: Dioptre, }\text{D})$$
  • Convex lens $\implies$ Positive power (e.g. $+2\text{ D}$).
  • Concave lens $\implies$ Negative power (e.g. $-1.5\text{ D}$).
  • Human Eye Defects & Correction:
  • Myopia (Short-sightedness): Can see near objects clearly, cannot see distant objects. Image forms in front of retina. Corrected using Concave Lens of suitable power.
  • Hypermetropia (Long-sightedness): Can see distant objects clearly, cannot see near objects. Image forms behind retina. Corrected using Convex Lens of suitable power.

4. Electricity & Magnetism

  • Electric Current: $$I = \frac{Q}{t} \quad (\text{Current in Amperes }\text{A}, \; Q \text{ in Coulombs }\text{C})$$
  • Potential Difference: $$V = \frac{W}{Q} \quad (\text{Voltage in Volts }\text{V}, \; W \text{ in Joules }\text{J})$$
  • Ohm's Law: $$V = IR \implies R = \frac{V}{I} \quad (\text{Resistance in Ohms, }\Omega)$$
  • Resistance & Resistivity: $$R = \rho \frac{l}{A}$$ ($l$ = length in $\text{m}$, $A$ = cross-sectional area in $\text{m}^2$, $\rho$ = resistivity in $\Omega\cdot\text{m}$)
  • Resistors in Series: $$R_s = R_1 + R_2 + R_3 \quad (\text{Current } I \text{ remains same, voltage divides})$$
  • Resistors in Parallel: $$\frac{1}{R_p} = \frac{1}{R_1} + \frac{1}{R_2} + \frac{1}{R_3} \quad (\text{Voltage } V \text{ remains same, current divides})$$
  • Joule's Law of Heating: $$H = I^2 Rt = VIt = \frac{V^2}{R}t \quad (\text{in Joules})$$
  • Electric Power: $$P = VI = I^2 R = \frac{V^2}{R} \quad (\text{in Watts})$$
  • Commercial Unit of Electrical Energy: $$1\text{ kWh} = 1\text{ Unit} = 1000\text{ W} \times 3600\text{ s} = 3.6 \times 10^6\text{ Joules}$$
  • Domestic Circuit Wires:
  • Live Wire: Red or Brown (carries current at $220\text{ V}$)
  • Neutral Wire: Black or Light Blue (completes circuit at $0\text{ V}$)
  • Earth Wire: Green or Yellow (safety measure connected to metal casing)
  • Fleming's Left-Hand Rule (Electric Motor):
  • Thumb: Direction of Force / Motion
  • Forefinger: Direction of Magnetic Field
  • Middle Finger: Direction of Current

2. Acids, Bases & pH Indicators

  • pH Scale:
  • $\text{pH} < 7 \implies$ Acidic (e.g. Gastric juice $\text{pH} \approx 1.2$, Lemon juice $\approx 2.2$)
  • $\text{pH} = 7 \implies$ Neutral (Pure water, $\text{NaCl}$ solution)
  • $\text{pH} > 7 \implies$ Basic / Alkaline (Blood $\approx 7.4$, Milk of Magnesia $\approx 10$, $\text{NaOH} \approx 14$)
  • Indicator Color Changes:
  • Blue Litmus: Turns RED in acid; remains blue in base.
  • Red Litmus: Turns BLUE in base; remains red in acid.
  • Phenolphthalein: Colourless in acid; turns PINK in base.
  • Methyl Orange: RED in acid; YELLOW in base.

3. Metals & Metallurgy

  • Reactivity Series: $$\text{K} > \text{Na} > \text{Ca} > \text{Mg} > \text{Al} > \text{Zn} > \text{Fe} > \text{Pb} > [\text{H}] > \text{Cu} > \text{Hg} > \text{Ag} > \text{Au}$$ (Potassium and Sodium are stored under kerosene oil because they react vigorously with atmospheric oxygen and moisture)
  • Roasting vs Calcination:
  • Roasting: Heating sulphide ore in excess air below its melting point: $$2\text{ZnS} + 3\text{O}_2 \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2\text{ZnO} + 2\text{SO}_2$$
  • Calcination: Heating carbonate ore in absence or limited air: $$\text{ZnCO}_3 \xrightarrow{\Delta} \text{ZnO} + \text{CO}_2$$
  • Thermite Reaction: $$\text{Fe}_2\text{O}_3 + 2\text{Al} \to 2\text{Fe (molten)} + \text{Al}_2\text{O}_3 + \text{Heat} \quad (\text{Used to weld railway tracks})$$

4. Carbon and Its Compounds

  • Why Carbon forms millions of compounds:
  • Catenation: Unique property of carbon atoms to form long chains with other carbon atoms.
  • Tetravalency: Valency of 4; forms strong covalent bonds with 4 other atoms.
  • Homologous Series:
  • Alkanes (single bond): $C_n H_{2n+2}$ (Methane $\text{CH}_4$, Ethane $\text{C}_2\text{H}_6$, Propane $\text{C}_3\text{H}_8$)
  • Alkenes (double bond): $C_n H_{2n}$ (Ethene $\text{C}_2\text{H}_4$)
  • Alkynes (triple bond): $C_n H_{2n-2}$ (Ethyne / Acetylene $\text{C}_2\text{H}_2$)
  • Functional Groups:
  • Alcohol: $-\text{OH}$ (Ethanol $\text{C}_2\text{H}_5\text{OH}$)
  • Aldehyde: $-\text{CHO}$ (Ethanal $\text{CH}_3\text{CHO}$)
  • Ketone: $>\text{C=O}$ (Propanone / Acetone $\text{CH}_3\text{COCH}_3$)
  • Carboxylic Acid: $-\text{COOH}$ (Ethanoic / Acetic Acid $\text{CH}_3\text{COOH}$)
  • Saponification (Soap Making): $$\text{Ester (Fat/Oil)} + \text{NaOH} \to \text{Soap (Sodium salt of fatty acid)} + \text{Glycerol}$$
  • Soap Cleansing Action:
  • Soap molecule has two ends:
    • Hydrophilic ionic head: Attracted to water (points outward).
    • Hydrophobic hydrocarbon tail: Attracted to oil/dirt (points inward).
  • Together they form spherical clusters called Micelles, trapping oil droplets to be rinsed away with water.

PART 3: BIOLOGY CORE CONCEPTS & GENETICS

1. Life Processes & Physiology

  • Photosynthesis Equation: $$6\text{CO}_2 + 12\text{H}_2\text{O} \xrightarrow[\text{Chlorophyll}]{\text{Sunlight}} \text{C}_6\text{H}_{12}\text{O}_6 + 6\text{O}_2 + 6\text{H}_2\text{O}$$
  • Breakdown of Glucose by Various Pathways: $$\text{Glucose (6C)} \xrightarrow{\text{in Cytoplasm}} \text{Pyruvate (3C)} + \text{Energy}$$
  • (a) In absence of $\text{O}_2$ (Yeast / Fermentation): $\to \text{Ethanol} + \text{CO}_2 + \text{Energy (2 ATP)}$
  • (b) In lack of $\text{O}_2$ (Muscle cells): $\to \text{Lactic acid} + \text{Energy (Causes muscle cramps)}$
  • (c) In presence of $\text{O}_2$ (Mitochondria / Aerobic): $\to \text{CO}_2 + \text{H}_2\text{O} + \text{Energy (38 ATP)}$
  • Double Circulation in Human Heart:
  • Blood passes through the heart twice in one complete cardiac cycle:
    1. Pulmonary Circulation: Deoxygenated blood from right ventricle $\to$ Lungs $\to$ Oxygenated blood to left atrium.
    2. Systemic Circulation: Oxygenated blood from left ventricle $\to$ Body organs $\to$ Deoxygenated blood to right atrium.
  • Nephron (Excretory Unit of Kidney):
  • Composed of: Bowman's capsule, Glomerulus, Renal tubule, Collecting duct.
  • Three steps of urine formation:
    1. Ultrafiltration (Glomerular filtration)
    2. Selective reabsorption (Glucose, amino acids, salts, water)
    3. Tubular secretion.

2. Genetics & Heredity

  • Mendel's Monohybrid Cross (Tall $\times$ Dwarf Pea Plant):
  • Parents: $TT \times tt$
  • $F_1$ Generation: All $Tt$ (All Tall)
  • $F_2$ Generation:
    • Phenotypic Ratio: $\mathbf{3 : 1}$ (3 Tall : 1 Dwarf)
    • Genotypic Ratio: $\mathbf{1 : 2 : 1}$ ($1\,TT : 2\,Tt : 1\,tt$)
  • Sex Determination in Humans:
  • Female gametes (Eggs) carry only $X$ chromosome ($22 + X$).
  • Male gametes (Sperms) carry either $X$ or $Y$ chromosome ($22 + X$ or $22 + Y$).
  • If sperm carrying $X$ fertilizes egg $\to XX \implies$ Girl Child.
  • If sperm carrying $Y$ fertilizes egg $\to XY \implies$ Boy Child.
  • Conclusion: The father's sperm determines the sex of the baby with a $50\%$ probability for each gender.

3. Ecology & Environment

  • 10% Law of Energy Transfer (Lindeman's Rule):
  • Only $10\%$ of energy is transferred from one trophic level to the next.
  • $90\%$ of energy is lost as heat to the environment during metabolic processes.
  • Example: Sun ($10,000\text{ J}$) $\to$ Plants ($100\text{ J}$) $\to$ Deer ($10\text{ J}$) $\to$ Tiger ($1\text{ J}$).
  • Ozone Layer Depletion:
  • Ozone ($\text{O}_3$) in stratosphere shields Earth from harmful UV radiation.
  • Depleted by Chlorofluorocarbons (CFCs) from refrigerators and fire extinguishers. $$\text{CF}_2\text{Cl}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{UV}} \text{CF}_2\text{Cl} + \text{Cl}^\bullet$$ $$\text{Cl}^\bullet + \text{O}_3 \to \text{ClO}^\bullet + \text{O}_2$$
  • Biomagnification:
  • Increase in concentration of non-biodegradable toxic substances (like DDT, mercury) at progressively higher trophic levels of a food chain. Highest concentration accumulates in top carnivores / humans.
