2. Question Paper Pattern (85 Marks, 2½ Hours)

The exam paper contains 44 questions divided into two sections:

Section A: Objective Questions — 42 Marks (49.4% of the Paper!)

  • Q1 to Q17 (17 Questions × 1 Mark = 17 Marks): Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs). Direct formula values, basic concepts.
  • Q18 to Q27 (10 Questions × 2 Marks = 20 Marks): Objective Type (each question has two 1-mark subparts: Fill in the blanks, True/False, Match the column).
  • Q28 (1 Question × 5 Marks = 5 Marks): Case Study / Context question with five 1-mark subparts.

    Key Strategy: Scoring 35+ out of these 42 objective marks immediately guarantees passing with flying colours!

Section B: Subjective Questions — 43 Marks

  • Q29 to Q37 (9 Questions × 2 Marks = 18 Marks): Very Short Answer (VSA). (4 have internal choices).
  • Q38 to Q42 (5 Questions × 3 Marks = 15 Marks): Short Answer (SA). (2 have internal choices).
  • Q43 to Q44 (2 Questions × 5 Marks = 10 Marks): Long Answer (LA). (Both have internal choices).

3. The "Sure-Shot" 30 Marks Checklist

If you master just these 6 topics, you will easily secure 30–35 marks in Section B alone:

  1. Heights & Distances (Trigonometry) [5 Marks]:
  2. Always Q44 (Light House / Tower / Building with angles $30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ$).
  3. Graphical Solution of Linear Equations OR Circle Tangents Construction [5 Marks]:
  4. Always Q43 (Plot two straight lines on graph sheet to find intersection, or construct tangents to circle from external point).
  5. Median / Mean of Grouped Data [3 Marks]:
  6. Always Q41 or Q42 (Using the median formula $L + \frac{\frac{N}{2} - cf}{f} \times h$).
  7. Coordinate Geometry [3 to 5 Marks]:
  8. Distance formula, Mid-point formula, and Section formula dividing line segment.
  9. Arithmetic Progression (AP) [3 to 4 Marks]:
  10. Finding the $n$-th term ($a_n = a + (n-1)d$) and sum of $n$ terms ($S_n$).
  11. Probability [3 Marks]:
  12. Simple problems on single/double dice, coin toss, or deck of 52 cards.
