2. Question Paper Pattern (85 Marks, 2½ Hours)

The written paper contains 43 questions totaling 85 Marks (+15 marks practical = 100 marks total):

Section A: Objective Questions — 42 Marks (49.4% of the Paper!)

  • Q1 to Q17 (17 Questions × 1 Mark = 17 Marks): Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs). Formula definitions, SI units, symbols, basic concepts.
  • Q18 to Q27 (10 Questions × 2 Marks = 20 Marks): Objective Type (each question has two 1-mark subparts: Fill in the blanks, True/False, Match the column).
  • Q28 (1 Question × 5 Marks = 5 Marks): Context / Case Study question with five 1-mark subparts.

    Scoring Insight: Getting 35+ on these 42 objective marks guarantees you pass easily without breaking a sweat!

Section B: Subjective Questions — 43 Marks

  • Q29 to Q37 (9 Questions × 2 Marks = 18 Marks): Very Short Answer (VSA). Definitions, 2-line reasoning, short calculations.
  • Q38 to Q42 (5 Questions × 3 Marks = 15 Marks): Short Answer (SA). Chemical reactions, ray diagrams, 3 differences.
  • Q43 (1 Question × 5 Marks = 5 Marks): Long Answer (LA) with internal choice (Human physiology or Physics laws/numerical).
  • Q44 (1 Question × 5 Marks = 5 Marks): Long Answer (LA) with internal choice (Electricity/Light or Carbon/Salts).

3. The 35-Mark "Sure-Shot" Topic List

If you master these 7 recurring topics, you will effortlessly score 35+ marks in Section B alone: 1. Light & Optics (5 Marks): Mirror/Lens formula numerical ($\frac{1}{f} = \frac{1}{v} \pm \frac{1}{u}$) and Myopia/Hypermetropia defects with ray diagrams. 2. Electricity (5 Marks): Ohm's law ($V = IR$), series vs parallel equivalent resistance, and electric power/energy billing numerical. 3. Chemical Salts (4 Marks): Preparation, chemical equations, and uses of Bleaching Powder, Baking Soda, Washing Soda, and Plaster of Paris. 4. Carbon Compounds (4 Marks): Saponification, cleansing action of soaps (micelles), and functional groups. 5. Life Processes (6 Marks): Nephron structure & function, Double circulation in human heart, and Aerobic vs Anaerobic respiration pathways. 6. Heredity (4 Marks): Mendel's monohybrid cross ($3:1$ phenotypic ratio) and sex determination in humans ($XX$ and $XY$). 7. Environment (4 Marks): 10% law of energy flow, Food chain vs food web, and Ozone layer depletion.
